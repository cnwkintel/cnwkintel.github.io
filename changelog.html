Changelog

November 20, 2026

2026 season data and website updates

Data

  • Updated club information and 2026 appearances, minutes, goals and assists for the 2003/04 through 2009/10 generations. Updated FotMob ratings and detailed statistics where available.
  • The reports cover 455 players, including 395 across the four generations and 60 from the 2011+ watchlist.

Website

  • Added the All Generations Report to the homepage, with combined charts, rankings and player profiles.
  • The combined Player Profiles can be filtered by generation, position and standing, and sorted by potential, FotMob rating, appearances or minutes. The Leader filter includes 2011+ players marked Watch.
  • Grouped chart segments open the corresponding player list. Individual points, bars and ranking entries link to player reports.
  • Impact Ranking defaults to adjusted impact (Adj), highest first. Potential ranges are sorted by their midpoints.
  • Added available portraits to featured players and restored missing portraits in the combined directory. Player cards show generation, standing and scouted potential where available.
  • Improved spacing and wrapping for chart labels and tooltips on smaller screens.
